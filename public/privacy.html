What we collect

Your location is used in your browser to show nearby places, directions and alerts. We do not store your location on our servers. Hazard reports you submit are currently saved only on your own device.

Third-party services

To show maps, search and directions, your browser sends map positions or search text to: OpenFreeMap (map tiles), Photon by Komoot (search), the Overpass API (nearby places, OpenStreetMap data) and OSRM (directions). Alerts come from data.gov.my.

Your rights (PDPA 2010)

You can clear all data NAVMAP keeps by clearing this site's data in your browser. Contact: [YOUR EMAIL].

Alerts disclaimer

NAVMAP is not an official warning service. Alerts are taken from public data (JPS, MetMalaysia via data.gov.my) and may be delayed or incomplete. Always follow instructions from NADMA, APM, JPS and MetMalaysia. In an emergency call 999.
