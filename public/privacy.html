In short

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

NAVMAP is a free navigation and live-alert map for Malaysia, available at navmap.vercel.app and as an installable web app (together, the "Service"). The Service is owned and operated by Yush Abdullah ("we", "us", "our"), who is the data user responsible for your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Service processes, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and what rights you have. It is written to meet the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia ("PDPA") and follows its seven principles: General, Notice and Choice, Disclosure, Security, Retention, Data Integrity and Access. Please read it together with our Terms of Service.

Words we use

  • Personal data: information that relates to you and can identify you, directly or together with other information.
  • Processing: collecting, recording, holding, using, sharing or deleting data.
  • Community pin: a short public report (for example "Heavy rain" or "Flood") that a user places on the map.
  • Device code: a random code created by your browser so the Service can recognise pins made on your device. It does not contain your name, phone number or any account.

2. What we collect and why, feature by feature

2.1 Your location

What: your GPS position, or an approximate position from your network, only after your browser or phone asks for permission and you allow it.

Why: to centre the map on you, list nearby places by distance, show the weather and pollution index for your area, warn you about alerts near you, give directions and turn-by-turn voice guidance, and show your coordinates in the SOS window.

How it is handled: your location is processed inside your browser. It is not sent to or stored on our servers, except as the rounded coordinates needed to request data for an area (see section 3). If you refuse or later turn off location permission, the Service still works using the centre of the map.

2.2 Searching for places

What: the words you type in the search bar and the area of the map you are viewing.

Why: to find matching places in Malaysia. The search text goes to the Photon search service. We do not keep a search history.

2.3 Nearby places (EV, fuel, food, toilets, surau, hospitals, workshops)

What: the category you chose and approximate coordinates (rounded to about 1 km).

Why: to list places near you. Most results load from a public file of places that is the same for everyone, so no location is sent at all. If we need fresher data, the rounded coordinates go through our server to the OpenStreetMap Overpass service. Nothing that identifies you is attached.

2.4 Directions and voice guidance

What: your starting point and destination coordinates.

Why: to calculate driving routes. They are sent to the OSRM routing service. While you navigate, your position is compared with the route on your device to give the next instruction and reroute you. Voice guidance uses your phone's built-in speech engine, and nothing you hear is recorded or sent to us.

2.5 Weather, rain radar and pollution index

What: coordinates rounded to about 5 km.

Why: to request the forecast and air quality from Open-Meteo, and rain radar images from RainViewer. An area name (for example "Petaling, Selangor") is looked up from Photon to label the weather card.

2.6 Official alerts

Flood, weather and earthquake alerts are downloaded from the Government of Malaysia open data portal (data.gov.my) through our server. No personal data is sent to get them; which alerts are near you is worked out on your device.

2.6a Nature and climate news

Headlines in the News tab are collected by our server from public news feeds (Google News, Malay Mail, The Star, The Guardian, BBC, UN News and Mongabay). No personal data is sent to get them. When the news list shows a picture, your browser loads it directly from the publisher, which receives standard technical details such as your IP address. When you tap a headline, the article opens on the publisher's own website, where their privacy policy applies.

2.7 Community pins

What we store when you post a pin: the pin type, the coordinates where you placed it, the time it was created and when it expires, and a one-way hash (a scrambled fingerprint) of your device code.

Why: to show live weather and hazard reports to other users nearby, and so that only your device can remove your pin. Other users see the pin type, place and time, never who posted it.

How long: each pin deletes itself automatically when it expires, between 1 and 12 hours depending on its type, or earlier if you press Remove pin.

Spam protection: to stop abuse we count pins per network for one hour. For this we store a one-way hash of your IP address for up to 1 hour. We cannot turn the hash back into your IP address.

2.8 SOS emergency calls

Tapping a number in the SOS window hands the call to your phone's own dialler or to WhatsApp. We do not see, record or receive who you call or when. The "Copy" button copies your coordinates to your own clipboard only.

2.9 Settings stored on your device

Your browser's local storage keeps your language choice (English or Bahasa Melayu), your device code, your map choice (map or satellite), any Home and Work places you save, and your pins when the shared database is not available. A service worker stores a copy of the app so it opens faster and works with a weak signal. These stay on your device and are not sent to us.

2.10 Technical logs

Like every website, our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically records technical information when your browser requests a page: IP address, browser and device type, the page requested, time and error codes. These logs are used only to run, secure and fix the Service and are kept according to Vercel's retention period.

2.10a Visitor statistics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand how many people use NAVMAP. It records page views with the page address, referring website, country, device type, operating system and browser. It uses no cookies and does not store your IP address. Visitors are counted with an anonymous code that is recalculated every day, so we cannot follow you across days or identify who you are. We only see totals, for example "1,200 visitors from Malaysia today, 80% on mobile". We use these figures only to improve the Service.

Public visitor counter. The app shows a public line such as "1,240 visitors today · 18 live pins". To count a visit, our server turns your IP address and browser name, together with today's date and a secret key, into a one-way code and adds it to a daily counter in our database (Upstash). The counter (a "HyperLogLog") keeps only an approximate total, so the code cannot be read back, and your IP address is not saved. Because the date is part of the code, visits on different days cannot be linked. Daily totals are deleted after 40 days. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, your visit is not counted and the app only reads the totals.

2.11 Emails you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and handle your request, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

2.12 Email updates (optional)

What: if you choose to subscribe, we store your email address, the date and time you agreed, your language (English or Bahasa Melayu) and the exact consent wording you agreed to.

Why: to send you NAVMAP news, feature updates, safety tips and future NAVMAP campaigns. We only add you after you type your email and tick the consent box yourself. The box is never ticked for you.

Where and how long: in our cloud database (Upstash), until you unsubscribe. Only the owner of NAVMAP can export the list. We do not sell, rent or share it with other companies for their own marketing. If we use an email sending service in future, it will only process your address to deliver our emails.

Unsubscribe: any time, by tapping Subscribe → Unsubscribe an email in the app, using the unsubscribe link in any NAVMAP email, or by emailing us. Your address is then deleted from the list.

What we do not collect

Apart from the optional email updates above, we do not ask for or collect your name, IC or passport number, phone number, contacts, photos, payment details or account passwords. We do not knowingly process sensitive personal data (such as health, religious or political information) as defined in the PDPA. Please do not include personal details in community pins or emails unless needed.

4. Your consent and choices

  • By using the Service you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the Service.
  • Location: allow or block it at any time in your browser or phone settings.
  • Community pins: you choose whether to post one, and can remove it at any time.
  • Email updates: optional. You join only by ticking the consent box, and can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Everything on your device: clear this site's data in your browser settings to delete your settings, device code and offline copy.
  • You may withdraw your consent at any time by stopping use of the Service, clearing site data and, if needed, emailing us.

5. How we protect your data

  • The whole Service is served over encrypted HTTPS.
  • Device codes and IP addresses are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Database access keys are server secrets and are never sent to your browser.
  • Pins carry no name, account or contact detail, and expire automatically.
  • We limit how many pins a network can post to prevent abuse.

No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure. If we become aware of a data breach that affects you, we will act quickly to contain it and inform affected users and the authorities where the law requires.

7. Keeping data accurate

Community pins are short-lived on purpose so that old reports disappear. If your pin is wrong, remove it with Remove pin. If you see a pin that is false or abusive, email us and we will remove it.

8. Your rights

Under the PDPA you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct data that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date;
  • withdraw consent to processing;
  • ask us to stop or limit processing that causes you damage or distress; and
  • ask us to stop using your data for direct marketing at any time. Unsubscribing from email updates does this immediately.

How to ask: email muhdyusry.abdullah@gmail.com with the subject "NAVMAP personal data request". Because the Service has no accounts, we usually cannot tell who you are, so please include details such as the pin type, the time and the place you posted it. We will reply within 21 days, as the PDPA requires. We may need to confirm the request comes from you before acting on it.

Complaints: if you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Department (Jabatan Perlindungan Data Peribadi, JPDP) at www.pdp.gov.my.

9. Cookies and similar technology

NAVMAP does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and does not use tools such as Google Analytics or Facebook Pixel. Visitor statistics come from Vercel Web Analytics, which works without cookies (see section 2.10a). It uses local storage and a service worker only for the purposes in section 2.9. Third-party providers listed in section 3 may receive standard technical information when your browser loads their content.

10. Children

The Service is meant for the general public and is not aimed at children under 13. Children should use it with a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, email us and we will delete it.
