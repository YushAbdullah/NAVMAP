Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026 · Operator: Yush Abdullah ("we", "us"), Malaysia

Summary. NAVMAP has no accounts, no adverts and no tracking cookies. Your location stays in your browser and is never saved by us. If you post a community pin, we keep its type, place and time until it expires (1 to 12 hours). We do not sell your data.

This policy explains how NAVMAP (the website at navmap.vercel.app and its installable web app, "the Service") processes personal data. It follows the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia (PDPA) and its seven principles: General, Notice and Choice, Disclosure, Security, Retention, Data Integrity and Access.

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or IC number, and we do not knowingly process sensitive personal data as defined in the PDPA.

2. Notice and choice

  • Location is only used after your browser asks you and you allow it. You can say no or turn it off at any time in your browser or phone settings. The map will then use its centre instead.
  • Community pins are only created when you press a pin button. Do not include anything that identifies you or another person.
  • By using the Service you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using it.

3. Disclosure to third parties

To work, your browser or our servers send the map area, search text or coordinates (not your name) to these service providers. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • OpenFreeMap: map tiles
  • Photon by Komoot: place search and area names
  • Overpass API mirrors (OpenStreetMap data): nearby places
  • OSRM: driving directions
  • Open-Meteo: weather and air-quality data
  • RainViewer: rain radar images
  • data.gov.my (Government of Malaysia): flood, weather and earthquake alerts
  • Vercel Inc.: website hosting
  • Upstash Inc.: the community pin database
  • Google Fonts: the text font

Transfer outside Malaysia. Some of these providers process data on servers outside Malaysia. By using the Service you consent to this transfer, as allowed under section 129 of the PDPA. We only share what each provider needs.

We may also disclose data when required by Malaysian law, a court order or a lawful request from the authorities. We never sell or rent personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Security

The site is only served over HTTPS. Device codes and IP addresses are stored as one-way hashes. Database keys are kept as server secrets and are never sent to your browser. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable practical steps to protect data from loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

5. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purpose in section 1. Community pins delete themselves when they expire, and the rate-limit record after 1 hour.

6. Data integrity

Community pins are short-lived on purpose so that stale reports disappear. If a pin is wrong, the device that posted it can remove it at any time.

7. Your rights and access

  • Remove your pins at any time with Remove pin in the app.
  • Delete everything NAVMAP stores on your device by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • Under the PDPA you may ask to access or correct your personal data, or withdraw consent. Because we do not know who you are, we can usually only act on a request that includes the pin or the time and place it was posted.

8. Cookies and local storage

NAVMAP does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It uses your browser's local storage for the settings in section 1, and a service worker to load the app faster and work with a weak signal.

9. Children

The Service is for the general public and is not aimed at children under 13. Children should use it with a parent or guardian.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change, and important changes will be shown in the app.

11. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints about personal data: open an issue at github.com/YushAbdullah/NAVMAP/issues (do not post personal details there; ask for a private contact instead). If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Department (JPDP) of Malaysia at pdp.gov.my.
