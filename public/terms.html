4. Alerts, weather and pollution index

  • Official alerts are taken from public government data on data.gov.my. They may be delayed, filtered to your area, incomplete or temporarily unavailable. When the live feed cannot be reached, the app may show clearly labelled SAMPLE alerts, which are examples and not real events.

  • The pollution index is an estimate from a computer model (Copernicus CAMS, via Open-Meteo) on the US AQI scale. It is not the official Air Pollutant Index (API / IPU) published by the Department of Environment Malaysia. For official readings, check the DOE APIMS website.
